# Design System: Lulur

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "A quiet reception desk"**

Lulur has three audiences:
- A **guest** booking on a phone, maybe on holiday. They need calm, photographs and an obvious next step.
- **Front-desk staff** who keep the calendar open all day and must see who is where, in which room, at a glance.
- **Therapists** checking their next client between sessions.

The booking pages are editorial and soft: Literata headings, real treatment photos, a step indicator, and a sticky summary that always shows the total and the deposit due today. The app is calm but dense where it matters (the calendar), with a light 76px rail that has labels under its icons, so non-technical staff never have to memorise icons.

Navigation follows docs/IA.md:
- Ten admin modules at the final phase.
- A calendar that needs every pixel of width.
- Hence a labeled light rail rather than a wide sidebar, an icon-only rail or top tabs.
- The location picker and date sit in the calendar header.
- Booking details open in a right panel so the day stays visible.

## Colors
- **Rosewood** #8C3B4A for primary actions, selected chips, days and slots, the current rail item, and the "now" line; strong #722E3B; soft #F6E6E9.
- **Gold** #B7791F marks packages and signature treatments; text uses #8A5A12 for contrast.
- **Neutrals:** a faint rose-grey ground #F5F3F4, white cards.
- **Calendar statuses** use a tinted fill, a 1px border in the status colour and a status icon. The word is in the legend, the tooltip and screen-reader text, because narrow blocks cut it off:
  - confirmed: blue
  - checked in: amber
  - in service: green
  - completed: grey
  - deposit pending: dashed rosewood
  - no-show: red
- Off-shift time is hatched (functional, not decorative).

## Typography
- Literata (600) for booking-page headings, service names, the tenant logo and the booking panel title.
- Hanken Grotesk for all UI.
- Booking body is 15px; app body is 14px; tables 13px; captions and calendar meta 12px; rail labels 11/600.
- Times and prices use tabular figures.

## Layout
- **Booking:** max 1120px; content plus a 340px sticky summary. Below 900px the summary becomes a fixed bottom bar showing the total, the deposit and the CTA.
- **Admin:** 76px rail plus content (max 1400px).
- **Calendar:** full width, a 56px time column plus therapist columns (minimum 132px, scrolling sideways on small screens), and a 360px detail panel that stacks below the calendar under 1280px.
- **Below 760px:** the rail becomes a top bar with a menu.
- **Therapist view:** single column, max 420px, with bottom tabs.

## Elevation & Depth
Cards float on a soft two-layer shadow with no border, the opposite of Roster's flat bordered cards. Popovers and toasts use a deeper shadow. Selected service cards and calendar blocks get a 2px rosewood ring.

## Shapes
2 (thin bars), 4 (small tags), 5 (avatar marks), 6 (list items, segments), 8 (buttons, inputs, calendar blocks), 10 (tenant mark), 12 (cards, option cards, service photos), 16 (hero), full (chips, avatars, switches, day dots).

## Components
- **Booking:** hero with a photo and scrim, a full-width party-size toggle (Just me / For two; solo hides couples-only treatments) above a one-line, swipeable category row that fades at the edge and hides itself when there is a single category, service cards (photo, Literata name, tag, duration chips, price, Add), add-on checkboxes, therapist option cards, a 7-day strip with availability dots, a slot grid grouped by part of day, health questions as Yes/No segmented controls, signature pad, policy list, payment option cards, sticky summary.
- **Calendar:**
  - Header: location, date, view toggles.
  - A legend row.
  - Therapist column heads showing level and booked hours.
  - Status blocks with a room line, plus a linked-partner line for couples.
  - Flags for missing forms, packages and walk-ins.
  - A now line.
  - Detail panel: guests with form status, resources, payment, tip presets, checkout.
- **Client profile:** stats, preferences with warnings, forms with validity, a package progress bar (used / booked / left), history, internal notes.
- **Service editor:** duration and price table with a master-therapist surcharge, guests, room-type listbox, skills checklist, booking-rule triggers and switches.
- **Reports:** utilisation bars, room-by-hour heat map in rosewood steps, tips table.
- **Custom controls only:** listbox, checkbox, radio, switch, chips as radios, segmented control, option card, date grid, signature pad. See mockups/components.html.

## Do's and Don'ts
### Do:
- **Do** show only bookable slots and explain why others are missing (suite full, day full, waitlist).
- **Do** show the deposit due today and the cancellation deadline before payment.
- **Do** surface allergies and form status wherever a therapist or front desk will see the client.
- **Do** pair every status colour with an icon, plus a word wherever there is room (badges, panels, legend).
### Don't:
- **Don't** use native browser or OS form controls.
- **Don't** use a left accent stripe as the only status signal on calendar blocks.
- **Don't** use cream or beige grounds; keep the cool rose-grey.
- **Don't** show staff photos or names that imply real people; demo staff are fictional.

## As built (2026-11-04)
Differences between this document and the shipped UI, so the next person does not trust the wrong thing:
- **Brand mark:** two overlapping circles whose lens is filled rosewood (therapist ∩ room = the free slot). Files: `public/brand/lulur-mark.svg`, `lulur-logo.svg`, `public/favicon.svg`; Vue component `LulurMark.vue`. Booking pages carry a small "Booking by Lulur" footer; the tenant's own letter mark stays in the header.
- **Fonts are self-hosted** (`@fontsource-variable/hanken-grotesk`, `@fontsource-variable/literata`); no request leaves the app for fonts.
- **Custom controls added:** `Pick.vue` (listbox, replaces every native select, including the time-of-day fields), `DatePick.vue` (date grid with keyboard: arrows, PageUp/PageDown, Enter, Esc). A DOM scan of every admin and booking page finds zero native select/date/time/checkbox/radio controls.
- **Calendar:** the 360 px detail panel is only reserved while a booking is selected; the grid otherwise uses the full width.
- **Known visual limits:** the calendar is a scrolling day grid, not a week view; admin on a phone shows the calendar with horizontally scrolled columns; the demo's "in service" and "checked in" blocks are seeded for daytime, so they look odd when the demo runs at night.
